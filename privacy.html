PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Privacy Principles

Your privacy is paramount. This website (jamshedali.me) is designed to operate with minimal data collection and zero invasive third-party ad tracking.

2. Data Collection & Use

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. The only circumstances under which information is received include:

  • Direct Contact: When you send an email or WhatsApp message, we receive your contact address, name, and message content solely to respond to your inquiry.
  • Server Access Logs: Standard HTTP server logs (IP address, user-agent, requested URL, timestamp) are retained temporarily for security auditing, anomaly detection, and operational integrity.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. A single localStorage flag (jamshed-motion) is utilized solely to remember your preferred animation settings (reduced motion vs. standard cinematic motion).

4. External Links & Third-Party Services

This website may link to third-party services such as GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Drive, or YouTube for video previews. Those external platforms maintain their own independent privacy policies.

5. Agentic AI & Machine-Readable Access

We publish structured files (llms.txt, index.md, /.well-known/mcp/server-card.json) to provide transparent, token-efficient, and ethical access for AI agents and search indexing tools.
